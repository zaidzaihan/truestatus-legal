# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

StatusCraft is an Android app made by Veilyo Studio ("we", "us"). It gets your videos ready for WhatsApp Status and puts them in a private WhatsApp group, so you can post them from there. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

The short version: StatusCraft has no servers and no accounts. Your videos are processed on your phone and go from your phone to your own WhatsApp. We never receive your videos, your chats, your contacts or your phone number.

## What StatusCraft handles, and where it stays

**Videos you pick.** You choose a video with Android's own picker. StatusCraft only gets the videos you pick, never the rest of your gallery. It prepares the video on your phone (changing its size, quality, length if you trim it, and adding the StatusCraft mark on free videos). Working copies are kept in the app's private storage and replaced the next time you send. If you tap "Save a copy to gallery", a copy is saved to your phone's Movies folder.

**Your WhatsApp link.** To put videos into WhatsApp, StatusCraft links to your WhatsApp account as a linked device, the same way WhatsApp on a computer does. It connects straight from your phone to WhatsApp's servers. The keys that keep this link working are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone.

While linked, StatusCraft:

- creates or reuses one group named "StatusCraft" where you are the only member,
- sends the videos you choose to that group, with the caption you write,
- does not download your chat history (this is switched off),
- does not read, store or send anywhere your chats, contacts, calls or other messages.

**Your phone number.** You type it in once to get a linking code. It is sent to WhatsApp to create that code, and kept on your phone so it's filled in if you link again. It is never sent to us.

**Your settings.** Things like whether you've seen the welcome screen, how many free videos without the mark you have left, and whether you've bought StatusCraft forever are stored on your phone.

## Who else is involved

**WhatsApp (Meta).** The videos and captions you send, and your phone number when linking, go to WhatsApp, because that's where you asked for them to go. WhatsApp handles them under its own terms and privacy policy. StatusCraft is not made by, or connected to, WhatsApp or Meta.

**Google Play.** If you buy StatusCraft forever, Google Play handles the payment. We don't see your card or payment details. Google tells the app whether your Google account owns the purchase, so it works again after a reinstall or on a new phone. Google handles this under its own privacy policy.

That's everyone. StatusCraft has no analytics, no crash reporting service, no advertising and no tracking. If we ever add ads to the free version, we will update this policy and tell you in the app before it happens.

## Permissions the app asks for

- **Internet:** to connect to WhatsApp and Google Play.
- **Notifications:** to show sending progress while you're in another app. You can say no; sending still works.
- **Foreground service:** to keep sending when you switch to WhatsApp.
- **Storage (Android 9 and older only):** to save a copy to your gallery when you ask.

## Keeping and deleting your information

Because everything stays on your phone, you're in control of it:

- **Unlink** in StatusCraft's Settings, or in WhatsApp under Linked devices. This ends the link and deletes its keys from the app.
- **Uninstall** StatusCraft, or clear its storage in Android settings, to delete everything it kept on your phone.
- Videos already sent to your StatusCraft group stay in your WhatsApp until you delete them there.

We can't delete or hand over your data on request, because we never have it.

## Children

StatusCraft is not meant for children under 13, or under the minimum age to use WhatsApp where you live.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects how your information is handled, we'll tell you in the app.
